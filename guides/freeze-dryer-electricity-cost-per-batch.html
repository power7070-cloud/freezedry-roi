How much electricity does a home freeze dryer use per batch?

Roughly $1.50–$4 per batch for most households. Home freeze dryers commonly run 20–40 hours per batch and average around 0.4–0.9 kW, which works out to very roughly 10–25 kWh per batch depending on unit size, load and how warm the room is.

At 15¢/kWh that is about $1.50–$3.75 per batch; Texas rates near 14¢ land in the same range. A cooler room and pre-frozen food shorten cycles. Use the calculator to plug in your own rate and batches per month.

Estimates only; check your unit's manual and nameplate wattage.

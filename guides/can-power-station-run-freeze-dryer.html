Can a solar generator or power station run a freeze dryer?

Only very large systems. A home freeze dryer can pull up to ~1,500 W at peak (needing a dedicated 15–20 A circuit) and uses on the order of 10–25 kWh over a full batch.

A 1–2 kWh portable station would run it only a couple of hours. Finishing a batch off-grid realistically needs a whole-home battery (10+ kWh) or a battery plus substantial solar. Don't start a batch on battery during an outage unless you can finish it, since interrupted cycles can spoil the load.

Estimates only; check your unit's manual and nameplate wattage.

FAQ

How much electricity does a freeze dryer use? A medium unit draws roughly 1–1.5 kW average over a 20–40 hour batch, about 30–40 kWh, or ~$4–6 at $0.15/kWh.

How long to pay off? For a family running 2–4 batches/month replacing store-bought freeze-dried food, payback is typically 1–3 years.
